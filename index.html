Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the NUVEXA Budget personal finance and budgeting application.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

NUVEXA Budget ("NUVEXA Budget", "the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a personal finance and budgeting application designed to help users record income and expenses, manage budgets, review financial activity, and monitor spending progress.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how that information is stored and used, and what choices you have when using NUVEXA Budget.

1. Information Stored by the App

NUVEXA Budget allows you to enter and manage information such as:

💰

Transactions

Income, expenses, amounts, titles, categories and dates.

📊

Budget Information

Budget limits, totals and spending progress.

👤

Profile

Optional profile information you choose to provide.

🔔

Preferences

Notification and application settings.

The information you enter is used to provide the budgeting and financial-management features of the App.

2. Local Data Storage

NUVEXA Budget is designed to store your budgeting information locally on your device.

The App uses local database and storage functionality for information required by features such as:

  • Transaction records
  • Income and expense totals
  • Categories
  • Budget information
  • Profile information
  • Application settings
  • Notification preferences

Based on the current functionality of the App, your financial records are not automatically uploaded to a NUVEXA-operated cloud database.

3. Financial Information

NUVEXA Budget may contain financial information that you personally enter, including income amounts, expense amounts, budgets, transaction descriptions, and spending categories.

NUVEXA Budget does not currently connect to your bank account or financial institution.

The App does not require you to provide bank account passwords, credit-card passwords, online-banking credentials, or similar authentication credentials.

NUVEXA Budget is a budgeting and record-keeping tool. It does not provide banking, investment, tax, accounting, or financial-advisory services.

4. Personal Information

The App may allow you to enter optional profile information, such as a name or nickname.

NUVEXA Budget does not currently require you to create an online account.

The App does not require an email address, telephone number, or password to use its core budgeting functionality.

If you voluntarily enter personal information into the App, that information may be stored locally on your device as part of your application data.

5. Notifications and Reminders

NUVEXA Budget may provide local notifications and reminders related to budgeting or financial activity.

Notification schedules and related preferences are configured and scheduled locally on your device.

The App does not require a remote push-notification server for these local reminders.

You can control notification permissions through your device's operating-system settings and through notification settings available within the App.

6. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored in NUVEXA Budget is used to provide and operate the functionality of the App.

Transaction information may be used locally to calculate:

  • Total income
  • Total expenses
  • Current balance
  • Budget usage
  • Category totals
  • Spending progress
  • Financial summaries and charts

These calculations are performed for the functionality of the App and do not mean that your financial information is being sent to an external financial service.

7. Data Sharing

Based on the current functionality of NUVEXA Budget, we do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your financial records with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties for advertising purposes.

The App does not currently provide your transaction history to third-party advertising networks.

If future versions introduce third-party services, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, account systems, or other features that materially change how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Third-Party Software

NUVEXA Budget may use third-party software libraries and platform services to provide features such as:

  • Local database storage
  • Local notifications
  • File creation or export
  • Image selection
  • User-interface functionality

These components are used to provide functionality within the App.

The use of a software library does not necessarily mean that your financial records are transmitted to the developer or to a third party.

9. Data Security

NUVEXA Budget is designed to keep application information on your device.

However, no digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

We recommend using appropriate device security measures such as:

  • A device screen lock or passcode
  • Current operating-system updates
  • Trusted application sources
  • Protection against unauthorized device access

10. Data Deletion

Because NUVEXA Budget primarily stores application information locally, you can delete information using the deletion functionality available within the App.

You may also remove the App from your device.

Depending on your device and operating system, uninstalling the App may remove its locally stored application data.

If you export financial information to files outside the App, deleting NUVEXA Budget does not necessarily delete those exported files.

11. Exported Data

If NUVEXA Budget provides an export feature, exported information may be saved as a file on your device or in a location selected by you.

Once information has been exported, the handling and security of that exported file depends on the storage location and services you choose to use.

If you manually share an exported file through email, cloud storage, messaging applications, or another service, that service may process the file according to its own privacy policy.

12. Children's Privacy

NUVEXA Budget is a general-purpose personal finance application.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a remote online account or cloud service.

Because the App is intended for personal financial management, parents or guardians should determine whether the App is appropriate for their children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of NUVEXA Budget, our data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated.

If a future version of NUVEXA Budget introduces accounts, cloud synchronization, online backup, analytics, advertising, financial-service integrations, or other features that change the way personal information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those practices.

15. Important Notice

NUVEXA Budget is intended to help users organize and monitor their own financial information.

The App does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, or banking advice.

Users are responsible for the accuracy of information they enter into the App and for maintaining the security of their device and any exported financial files.
